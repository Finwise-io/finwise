Naming: agreed — "Money" is everything. Recommendation: Cash flow — it's already the tab-bar name (no ripple through nav, menu, or approved docs), it honestly covers money in AND out (Budget would misname the income and debt tabs), and it's the term this audience uses. The redundancy is solved by renaming the first TAB to This month: Cash flow → This month · Income · Spending · Debts. (If you prefer "Budget" as the screen name, everything else stands — one word to change.) Teller-readiness is drawn in throughout: connect-your-bank doors with honest "coming soon", source chips on every transaction (bank · imported · by hand), and copy that promises automatic import without pretending it exists today.
