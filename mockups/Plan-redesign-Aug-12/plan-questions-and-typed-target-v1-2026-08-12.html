The same words have to land on three more screens — the parameters are asked in four places, and if only the Plan tab changes they will disagree:
  • Retirement (the cockpit) — “Retire at age”, “Plan to age”, and the compare table’s “Retire at”.
  • What drives this? (will it last) — “Retire age”, “Plan to age”.
  • What if I save more? — “Your age”, “Retire at”.

I would sweep all four in one go, so no two screens ask the same thing differently. Say the word and it's one change, not four.

Two things this decision settles from the audit.

D1 — “the target isn't the number that gives 80% odds” stops being a defect the moment the target is yours: your number owes nobody 80%. What survives is the suggestion we offer when you leave it blank — that one still has to be honest, and today it rounds itself under its own line.

D3 — “short $46,243 above a $371,000 ask” dissolves: the gap and the saving are both measured against your target now, so they are the same arithmetic.
