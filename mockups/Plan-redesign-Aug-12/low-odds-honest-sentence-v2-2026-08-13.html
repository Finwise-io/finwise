Two changes the verification forced.

1 · Phone numbers are now on the rows. Two of the three run staffed helplines and I did not know that when I drew v1. For someone in their late sixties who has just been told they cannot retire, a number to call is likely to beat a web form. It should not be hidden inside the sheet.

2 · SHIP goes to the locator, not the homepage. Help is state-by-state, so a generic landing page would make the person hunt. Send them to the finder.
