Where this design can fail — and the guard for each (including your Rocket story):
1. Transfers read as income or spending — the Rocket killer. Guard: broker records import as HISTORY ONLY (never spending); moves between your own accounts pair up as transfers; any large unmatched arrival gets the calm "Worth a look — was this you?" card and asks, never assumes.
2. Fake year-to-date — an account connected in July has no Jan 1. Guard: every window caps at your data start and says so ("since Jul 19").
3. Deposits inflating returns — the classic complaint. Guard: money-weighted math + the standing sentence "deposits never count as return" + the dollar breakdown beside every percent.
4. Missing activity looking like zero dividends — absence of records ≠ $0 earned. Guard: per-account coverage notes name what the broker didn't share; file imports always say "no activity records."
5. Two "+$2,110"s meaning different things — your sketch had net-worth change equal investment return by coincidence; real users will conflate them. Guard: the what-drove-it split on Net worth + different window labels keep them visibly distinct.
