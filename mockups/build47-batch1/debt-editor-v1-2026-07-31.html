    Your finding, verified: the Cash-flow Debts editor asks only min payment + due day; the Net-worth editor asks more (min AND you-pay) — two editors drifted apart, and NEITHER captures a payoff end date or a due-in-full loan. A 5-month balloon loan is unrepresentable today — the closest fit (a monthly minimum) misstates reality.
  • 2
    The fix: ONE debt editor, shaped by how the debt is repaid. Installments get the real payment + end date (either entered, the other computed — one source of truth). Cards keep today's fields. Due-in-full gets amount + date and flows into Cash flow's big-ticket radar and the bill calendar for that month.
