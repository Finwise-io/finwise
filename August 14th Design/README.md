# August 14th Design — the live design set

**This folder is the single source of truth for the Plan and Home redesigns.** Anything not in here
is superseded. Created 2026-08-14 on founder instruction, after four overlapping copies of the same
Claude Design delivery had accumulated and nobody could tell which was current.

## What is here

| File | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `PLAN-REDESIGN-BRIEF-v3-2026-08-14.md` | The brief for the Plan surface. Carries every founder ruling to date, three personas with reconciling numbers, and the rules that cannot bend. | **LIVE — send this** |
| `HOME-REDESIGN-BRIEF-v1-2026-08-14.md` | The brief for the Home surface. Takes its figures from the Plan brief so the same person cannot wear different numbers on two screens. | **LIVE — send this** |
| `HANDOFF-REVIEW-Plan-v3-2026-08-13.md` | What is wrong with the Plan design that came back: six blockers, four untrue numbers, all five rulings now closed. | **LIVE — send this** |
| `Claude-Design-handoff-received-2026-08-13/` | Claude Design's own files, as delivered. Net worth, Performance and Plan. | **Their work — do not edit** |

Send all three documents together with their own folder, so the designer corrects their file rather
than redrawing from scratch.

## What is deliberately NOT here

- **Superseded briefs** — v1 and v2 of the Plan brief, and the 2026-08-12 handoff review, stay in
  `mockups/Plan-redesign-Aug-12/`. The mock-first rule keeps old versions; it does not keep them
  where they can be mistaken for current.
- **Earlier Claude Design deliveries** — three older copies stay in `A-instructions for claude/`.
  Compared byte for byte, only one file among them is unique: the superseded v2 Plan drawing.

## The decisions this set carries

Thirty-two founder decisions are recorded across these files and the code. They are verified against
the files rather than remembered — see the changelog tab of
`docs/FCC-core-55-70/FCC-core-detailed-design-v1.3-…xlsx`, which carries one dated row per decision
under the same-day changelog rule.

## Still open, and waiting on the designer rather than on us

Six questions are deliberately left for Claude Design to answer with a stated assumption, so the
founder rules on something concrete: three in the Plan brief §10 (whether extra saving survives as an
eighth row, whether the seven inputs save immediately or keep the draft-then-commit split, where the
suggested-target offer lives) and three in the Home brief §8 (how the chief-of-staff block and the
Insights screen relate, where Sharpen goes, and whether June's headline is what she can draw, does
draw, or her whole paycheck).
