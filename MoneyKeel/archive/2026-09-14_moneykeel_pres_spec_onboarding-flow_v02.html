The thing fixing the loop has exposed

The same two questions are asked twice, in two different vocabularies. Screen 2 asks your primary goals with four options and where you are with two. Part B opens by asking what this should help with using eleven options, and what sounds like you using four. Nobody noticed, because nobody could reach part B. Now that the door works, anyone who taps it is asked both again, differently — and the second answers overwrite the first.

That needs deciding before the door is put in front of people: either part B drops its first two screens and inherits what part A already knows, or part A's two questions become the same question part B asks.
