The order I would build it in

Two rules decide the order: a change that moves no number goes in alone and first, and a change that moves a number people will notice goes in where it can be seen and explained.

  1. Delete the gross-up branch and salaryMode. Not free after all. The plan called it dead code; salaryMode: 'takehome' is still written by IncomeSetupSheet and still selectable in the income manager, so the branch runs today. It can only go once the new base-pay screen replaces both. This moves behind step 4.
  2. The base-pay SCREEN, from the approved mock (2a–2e). The calculations behind it are done and pinned by 25 laws; what is left is the screen that captures a second earner — a section per person, the fourteen-month table inside it, and the household block. Until it exists, nothing can WRITE a partner’s wage.
  3. The filing-status question, and over-65 for a couple. Data plus a screen. Five surfaces get the right answer for the first time; a couple sees $328–$866 a month more.
  4. Dividends out of take-home. Last of the number-movers, because it is the only one that makes a figure SMALLER — every surplus drops by the dividend amount. People notice a reduction, so it lands on its own with the reason on the screen.
  5. Dividends from the holdings. Reaches nothing today; safe whenever.
