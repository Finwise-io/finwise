# audit

Verification records: device-test sheets for each build, audit findings and their outcomes, and
anything whose job is to CHECK the work rather than be the work. Created on the founder's ruling,
2026-09-01.

A test sheet is named for the build it verifies — `moneykeel_build_audit_device-test-build-54_v01.xlsx`
— and once that build is superseded the sheet keeps its name here: it is the record of what was
checked, not a living document. The founder's own filled-in copies follow his rule and carry `-jj`.

Never here: specifications (standards/), mockups (the domain folders), or anything the app reads.
