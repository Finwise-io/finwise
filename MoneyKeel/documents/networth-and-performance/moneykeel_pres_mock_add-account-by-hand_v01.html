Add an account — what it looks like today

This is the screen that “Add by hand” opens. It is drawn from the code: the words, fields, buttons and their order below are what the app shows today, for each of the six kinds of account you can pick at the top. The screen is one form that changes its fields when you pick a kind. The Save button stays faded until a name and a balance are typed, and then reads “Save $20,000” with the amount. Nothing on this page is a proposed change.
