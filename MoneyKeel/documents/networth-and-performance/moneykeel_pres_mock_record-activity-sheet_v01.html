The account page’s recorder — v01, drawn from the code

The sheet behind the account page’s first two doors (money in or out, and income received), and the amber box’s door for dividends. Drawn as built on 2026-10-22, in nine states; approval pending.

#WhatWhyDecision
1Drawn from src/screens/AccountPortfolioScreen.tsx and the sheet’s own code. Each state was rendered on Sep 30, 2026 with the accounts, records and prices listed for it in src/testing/sheetStates.tsx, and every word, size, weight and colour was read off that render. Colours are the tokens of src/utils/theme.ts, name for name.A drawing of what is built is taken from the code, never from an older drawing. This sheet was built with no drawing behind it.DEC-0470
2Under the sheet, dimmed, is the top of the page it opens over, down to the page’s first card. The page’s design is the account-page drawing v01; nothing of it is redrawn here.One design per screen. This drawing adds only the sheet.DEC-6457
3The phone’s own parts are drawn but not checked: the status bar and the orange state labels. The keyboard is not drawn. A sheet taller than the screen is drawn whole; on the phone it scrolls inside the sheet.The phone draws them, not MoneyKeel.—
4Status: drawn from the code, approval pending. 9 states, one phone, each state top to bottom.Every state of a sheet is drawn, including a fresh account.DEC-0470
5Two doors on the account page open it: the money door (deposit, withdraw, transfer) and the income door (dividend and interest; a bond’s interest and coupon; a cash account’s interest only, so no choice is shown, state I).Drawn as built; the doors are the account-page drawing v01’s own.DEC-6457
6The account is named as the page names it, in the title, the line before saving and the list of accounts to transfer into. Before, the sheet wrote the bare name, so a page headed by the institution and name opened a sheet with the name alone.One account name everywhere. Found drawing the sheet, 2026-10-22.accountDisplayNames (Build-47 walk row 22)
7The kinds and the two days are written in sentence case. Before, a style capitalised every word of a kind (coupon received).One way of writing a label. Found drawing the sheet, 2026-10-22.—
8The dark-green bar over the list of accounts to transfer into runs edge to edge across the sheet, with square corners (states D and E). Before, it sat inside the sheet’s padding.His standard for every dark-green bar.founder 2026-08-11 · 2026-09-14
9A deposit or a withdrawal on an account with holdings says where the money lands or comes from; the line before saving says what saving records; Save waits for an amount, and for a transfer, the account it goes to.Drawn as built.—
10The amount box is ready for typing when the sheet opens, so on the phone the keyboard rises and the sheet sits above it; the keyboard is not drawn.The phone draws it.—
11Noted, not changed: the day is today or yesterday only, and the transfer list offers every other account, a bond included.Drawn as built.—
12Held word for word, in order, with every line’s size, weight and colour, by src/screens/__tests__/sheets_drawn_from_code.test.tsx (the record-activity block). A change to the sheet that is not made here first turns that test red.A drawing nobody checks drifts from the app.—
