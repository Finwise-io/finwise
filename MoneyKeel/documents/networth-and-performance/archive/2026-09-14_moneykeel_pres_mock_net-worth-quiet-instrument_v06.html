Net worth — the screen as Build 53 shipped it

What this is. The picture of the Net worth screen exactly as Build 53 shipped it. Nothing on this page is a new design. Every word was taken by rendering the Build 53 code (commit 263f741) in each state, and the amounts are the ones that code printed for this household. Three things keep v5's figures, not the render: the down case in state B (−$1,480), the sample dates, and the change walk (state E), which the Build 53 code did not change.

Why it exists. Mock v5 (2026-08-11) was the last Net worth mock. The changes below were built into the app from 2026-08-11 to 2026-08-14 without a new mock. One of them was the square corners, on your direction "no mock up ... just build it". So no picture matched what shipped until this one.

What is different from v5 — all of it already in the app at Build 53:
  1. Every dark-green bar has square corners and runs edge to edge: your net worth, your retirement plan, what you own, what you owe and emergency cushion.
  2. The light-green group bars run edge to edge too, and their titles are in capitals, like the dark-green ones.
  3. Every bar total is 15 points, the same size as the row amounts, and sits on the same right edge.
  4. The retirement line leads with the odds: "78% odds of lasting to age 92 · retire at 65 with …". The word "Likely" is gone.
  5. Each account row carries its source: "✍️ By hand · you update it" or "🔗 Connected · updated today".
  6. By institution: the bar splits by institution, every institution opens, and the E*TRADE account is named "E*TRADE Brokerage" under the one account-naming rule.
  7. The debt bar uses one brown in lighter and darker steps, so it reads without colour vision.
  8. The banner names each gap in one line; the fix sheet's wording changed, and a connection row carries Reconnect beside Sync now.
  9. First day: the whole sample sentence, "Sample: 84% odds of lasting to age 90", is in the sample style.
Read with care. The retirement figures are what Build 53 printed for this household in the test run. The $4,300,000 and $11,418,000 come from the test household's salary and savings, which this page does not show. The dates are v5's sample dates. Rulings made after Build 53 are not drawn here, for example "Debts", "Emergency fund", "Nothing entered yet." and 20-point titles.
