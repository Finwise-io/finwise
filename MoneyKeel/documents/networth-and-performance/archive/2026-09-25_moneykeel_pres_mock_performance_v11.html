Mark 13 in full — what we can and cannot rebuild.
We can, for a linked account. On the first sync the broker sends its whole transaction history (the app asks with no start date), and it tells us the date of its own first record. Market data gives us three years of daily closing prices. Walk today’s holdings backwards through those transactions and price each day at its close, and you get a real value line back to the broker’s first record — the same history the broker shows you.
We cannot, and should not invent. A hand-entered account or a cash balance has no ledger: it honestly starts the day you enter it. A file import rebuilds only as far back as the file’s purchase dates.
A defect this exposes. The graph today takes today’s share count and prices it at past dates. Someone who bought last month is shown a year-long line that never happened, and it will not match their statement. Whatever you decide on the rebuild, this has to go: until a day is rebuilt from records, the line should not start before it.
My recommendation. Rebuild it, and say on the screen where the history came from and where it starts: “Rebuilt from E*TRADE’s records · Jun 3, 2024”, with a second line for anything that starts today. If you say yes I will draw day one again as a linked account, with the numbers, before any of it is built.
