The trade sheet — v03, drawn from the code

The sheet that records a buy, a sale, a deposit, a withdrawal, a transfer or a dividend for an account that holds stocks or funds. It opens from the account page’s third door and from a Holding page. Drawn as built on 2026-10-22, in twelve states; redrawn from the code on 2026-10-23 with the fee of a buy or a sale; approval pending.

5Two doors open it. On the account page of a stock or mixed account, the third door under ACTIVITY opens it with that account chosen (states A to K). On a Holding page, the button under the page opens it with that account chosen and the holding’s ticker filled in (state L).Gap 7, 2026-10-21: the account page’s door sent a stock account to the Performance tab, which has no way to record a trade. The Holding page’s ticker was lost the moment the sheet set its account; fixed the same day. To reverse the first: point the door back at the Performance tab.DEC-6457 (the door’s drawing) · DEC-2484g
6Six kinds, chosen at the top; Buy is chosen when it opens. Buy asks the ticker, the shares, the price and the day. Sell offers the account’s holdings to choose from and says how many you hold. Deposit and Withdraw ask an amount and a day; Transfer asks the account it goes to; a dividend is paid as cash or reinvested.Drawn as built.—
7The cash line under the account shows the cash the account holds. A buy, a withdrawal or a transfer bigger than it is refused, and the line says so in red (state C); a sale bigger than the holding is refused the same way (state E). Where the account’s cash was never entered the line says so and nothing is refused on it (state J).Unknown is not zero.DEC-3523 tenet 3 · MK-PF-M-020
8Bonds and alternatives are not recorded here: each one in the household is listed with a door to its own screen (states A to I).Every account has a way to record its sales.B46 finding 6 (approved 2026-07-31)
9The day picker stops at today.A record cannot be dated in the future.DEC-6824
10A fresh account (state J and K): nothing held and no cash entered. Buy opens ready; Sell says there is nothing to sell.Every state of a sheet is drawn, a fresh account included.DEC-0470

162026-10-25 · A buy of a ticker the account does not hold yet asks what it is, in the holding editor’s words and chips: “Type (sets the benchmark)”, then Money market · CD · Stocks / Funds · Fixed income · Crypto · Gold / commodities · Options · Annuities · Other, nothing chosen for the person. Until one is chosen the line under the chips reads “UNKNOWN123 isn’t held in this account yet — choose its type to record the buy.” and Record buy waits. Once one is chosen the question names its benchmark, as the editor’s does: “Type (sets the benchmark — SPY)”. Options adds the editor’s “Shares per contract” box, 100 unless changed, with its line “100 for a standard option; 10 for a mini option. Value and cost are multiplied by it.” The cash check and the cash itself count contracts × shares per contract × price: 1 contract at $2.50 is $250, so $1,000 of cash reads $750 after it (it read $997.50), and the new holding keeps its 100 a contract. The new holding is filed by the type chosen: 10 VTI chosen as Stocks / Funds list under Stocks / Funds. A ticker the account holds is not asked (states B, C and L, unchanged). This state is described here; the phone is not redrawn.Found by the outside review of 2026-10-25 (MK-CF-F-223 and MK-CF-F-138): a first buy of an option counted one share a contract, and a ticker nothing identifies was filed under Stocks / Funds by the classifier’s last-resort default. His rulings: an option is contracts × 100 × price unless the person states otherwise; when nothing says what a holding is, refuse the line and ask. The lead’s decision, made without asking him. To reverse: remove the question; a first buy then counts one share a contract and is filed by its ticker alone.DEC-0274 · DEC-4646 · DEC-2160 · DEC-7605
172026-10-25 · Sell, when a purchase of the holding has no share count on record: the line under the holdings reads “You own: Not stated — a purchase has no share count on record. ⚠ add the share count in Edit holding before recording a sale” in red, and Record sell waits (it read “You own shares ⚠ can’t sell more than you own”). A sale recorded another way on such a holding (the ✎ edit sheet, or a delete that applies later records again) takes shares oldest first only from purchases whose count is on record and stops at the one that has none: that purchase is kept as it was, and the holding’s Quantity keeps saying “Not stated — a purchase has no share count on record.” (the purchase was erased, and the holding disappeared).Unknown is not zero, and the person’s entry is never overwritten (DEC-3523 tenets 3 and 5; outside review MK-CF-F-139). The lead’s decision, made without asking him. To reverse: the old words, and a missing count read as 0 when a sale is taken.DEC-3523
182026-10-25 · In an account whose cash was never entered, Available cash stays “not entered” after money is recorded in or out of it — a buy, a sale, a deposit, a withdrawal, a dividend paid as cash — and nothing is refused on it (as in state J). The app keeps a running figure so the account’s total stays right, but that figure started from nothing on record, so it is not the cash the account holds. 100 VTI imported, no cash entered, 10 more bought at $100.00: the line reads “Available cash: not entered” before the buy and after it (after it, it read “Available cash: −$1,000 ⚠ not enough — deposit or transfer cash in first”, and the next buy was refused). A linked account’s cash is the broker’s figure and is shown as sent.Tenet 3: a number made from an unknown is not known cash (the lead’s ruling on the outside review’s open question, 2026-10-25). To reverse: let Available cash read the running figure again.DEC-3523 tenet 3 · MK-PF-M-020
192026-10-25 · A buy or a sale of an option — one the account holds (kind Options), or a first buy with Options chosen (row 16): the first box reads “Contracts”, and under the three boxes a line shows the shares in each contract and what the trade comes to: “Each contract is 100 shares: 1 × 100 × $2.50 = $250.” (before a figure is typed, “Each contract is 100 shares.”; a mini option, 10). The price box keeps “Price / share”: an option is quoted per share, as a broker’s order ticket and confirmation write it and as the app records it (“1 contract @ $2.50”); labelled per contract, $250 typed would count $25,000. Sell names the holding’s own unit: “You own 2 contracts” (it read “You own 2 shares”). The ✎ edit sheet of an option’s record on the account page reads “Contracts” the same way. It read “Shares” for an option everywhere.
