Rulings checked and not drawn — each needs your call or its own mock
  • The return on cash + investments, “+0.5%”. The precision ruling Q49 shows a return to two decimals, which would read +0.53%. The master row for this figure (MK-NW-M-002) says one decimal, and so does the app. One decimal or two?
  • “Tight ⚠” beside the emergency fund. Ruling R3 (2026-08-20) says to show the fact, not a grade. Keep it, or show only “2.0 months”?
  • “🔗 Connected · updated today”. Ruling R7 says to show the source always and the date only when it is stale, which would make it “🔗 Connected”. Keep the date?
  • “Connected” and “Reconnect”. They describe an account that is already linked, not a way to add one, so this page leaves them alone. Should they say “Linked” and “Link again”?
  • Liquidity tiers. Ruling D1 (Liquid / Sellable / Locked / Not spendable) is ruled but not designed. It needs its own mock.
  • Five full green bars. The general rule keeps the filled bar for groups with a total. Net worth keeps all five by your 2026-08-11 ruling, confirmed today with v06.
  • Screen title 20 points (Net worth has none), MoneyKeel at the top (already there), and the banned word “safe” (not on this screen): nothing to change.
