The return is read from the summary directly beneath, where it already is. You were right, and here is why it happened: the radius WAS 12, but the chip was only 26 points tall. Two more disagreements I found in today’s code while drawing this — the build fixes both.
(a) The summary Return does not count your income. It is worked out from price change only, then weighted across holdings; the “Wealth generated” figure beside it DOES count dividends and interest. On the final design’s example they happen to agree (+0.54% ≈ $1,910 ÷ $352,404); on real data they will not. The build uses one meaning throughout — wealth generated ÷ the money you had working — which is what the new phone shows: $6,904 ÷ $42,523 = +16.24%.
