The trade sheet — v01, drawn from the code

The sheet that records a buy, a sale, a deposit, a withdrawal, a transfer or a dividend for an account that holds stocks or funds. It opens from the account page’s third door and from a Holding page. Drawn as built on 2026-10-22, in twelve states; approval pending.

READ THIS FIRST — what this drawing is, what it was drawn from, and what changed while it was drawn
#WhatWhyDecision
1Drawn from src/screens/AccountPortfolioScreen.tsx and the sheet’s own code. Each state was rendered on Sep 30, 2026 with the accounts, records and prices listed for it in src/testing/sheetStates.tsx, and every word, size, weight and colour was read off that render. Colours are the tokens of src/utils/theme.ts, name for name.A drawing of what is built is taken from the code, never from an older drawing. This sheet was built with no drawing behind it.DEC-0470
2Under the sheet, dimmed, is the top of the page it opens over, down to the page’s first card. The page’s design is the account-page drawing v01 (state L: holding detail v02); nothing of it is redrawn here.One design per screen. This drawing adds only the sheet.DEC-6457
3The phone’s own parts are drawn but not checked: the status bar and the orange state labels. The keyboard is not drawn. A sheet taller than the screen is drawn whole; on the phone it scrolls inside the sheet.The phone draws them, not MoneyKeel.—
4Status: drawn from the code, approval pending. 12 states, one phone, each state top to bottom.Every state of a sheet is drawn, including a fresh account.DEC-0470
5Two doors open it. On the account page of a stock or mixed account, the third door under ACTIVITY opens it with that account chosen (states A to K). On a Holding page, the button under the page opens it with that account chosen and the holding’s ticker filled in (state L).Gap 7, 2026-10-21: the account page’s door sent a stock account to the Performance tab, which has no way to record a trade. The Holding page’s ticker was lost the moment the sheet set its account; fixed the same day. To reverse the first: point the door back at the Performance tab.DEC-6457 (the door’s drawing) · DEC-2484g
6Six kinds, chosen at the top; Buy is chosen when it opens. Buy asks the ticker, the shares, the price and the day. Sell offers the account’s holdings to choose from and says how many you hold. Deposit and Withdraw ask an amount and a day; Transfer asks the account it goes to; a dividend is paid as cash or reinvested.Drawn as built.—
7The cash line under the account shows the cash the account holds. A buy, a withdrawal or a transfer bigger than it is refused, and the line says so in red (state C); a sale bigger than the holding is refused the same way (state E). Where the account’s cash was never entered the line says so and nothing is refused on it (state J).Unknown is not zero.DEC-3523 tenet 3 · MK-PF-M-020
8Bonds and alternatives are not recorded here: each one in the household is listed with a door to its own screen (states A to I).Every account has a way to record its sales.B46 finding 6 (approved 2026-07-31)
9The day picker stops at today.A record cannot be dated in the future.DEC-6824
10A fresh account (state J and K): nothing held and no cash entered. Buy opens ready; Sell says there is nothing to sell.Every state of a sheet is drawn, a fresh account included.DEC-0470
11Noted, not changed: the dividend kind lists the cash accounts too (a savings account among them), and the sheet’s title names one record by a word the activity list does not use (canon row 12 words the list itself as activity).Drawn as built; each is a question of words for the canon’s owner, not a fault in a figure.canon row 12
12Held word for word, in order, with every line’s size, weight and colour, by src/screens/__tests__/sheets_drawn_from_code.test.tsx (the trade block). A change to the sheet that is not made here first turns that test red.A drawing nobody checks drifts from the app.—
