Alternatives — v01

Everything that is neither a listed share nor a bond: crypto, private equity, hedge funds, commodities, annuities, options. Each is held at a value you set, because none of them has a price feed — and the screen says so rather than implying one.

The screen is titled “Alternatives”, and its route is /other-investments. The heading and the dark-green bar both read ALTERNATIVES; only the address differs. Worth knowing when you are looking for it.

What “from Build 53” means. Build 53 shipped this at 263f741 and 38 lines have changed since. The visible ones are your rulings: “EST. GROWTH / YR” became “EST. GROWTH /year” and “%/yr expected” became “%/year expected” (the /month · /year canon), and returns moved from one decimal to two (the precision standard, Q49). Plus a refusal that did not exist before: where the growth cannot be worked out it now shows — rather than a zero.

The numbers add up. $28,000 + $25,000 + $8,500 = $61,500. The blended expected return is each holding's benchmark weighted by its value: (28,000 × 9.00% + 25,000 × 7.00% + 8,500 × 3.50%) ÷ 61,500 = 7.32%, and 7.32% of $61,500 is $4,502 a year — the figure shown.
