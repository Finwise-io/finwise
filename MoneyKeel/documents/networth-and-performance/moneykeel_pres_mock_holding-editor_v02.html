The holding editor — v02, drawn from the code

The sheet behind the Holding page’s ✎ Edit: a holding’s ticker, name, type, purchases and account, and for a bond its own facts. Drawn as built on 2026-10-22, in nine states; approval pending.

READ THIS FIRST — what this drawing is, what it was drawn from, and what changed while it was drawn
#WhatWhyDecision
1Drawn from src/screens/HoldingDetailScreen.tsx and the sheet’s own code. Each state was rendered on Sep 30, 2026 with the accounts, records and prices listed for it in src/testing/sheetStates.tsx, and every word, size, weight and colour was read off that render. Colours are the tokens of src/utils/theme.ts, name for name.A drawing of what is built is taken from the code, never from an older drawing. This sheet was built with no drawing behind it.DEC-0470
2Under the sheet, dimmed, is the top of the page it opens over, down to the page’s first card. The page’s design is holding detail v02; nothing of it is redrawn here.One design per screen. This drawing adds only the sheet.DEC-0650
3The phone’s own parts are drawn but not checked: the status bar and the orange state labels. The keyboard is not drawn. A sheet taller than the screen is drawn whole; on the phone it scrolls inside the sheet.The phone draws them, not MoneyKeel.—
4Status: drawn from the code, approval pending. 9 states, one phone, each state top to bottom.Every state of a sheet is drawn, including a fresh account.DEC-0470
5One door: ✎ Edit on a Holding page (holding detail v02 draws it). It edits a holding you have. Its mode for adding a new holding is gone.Gap 8, 2026-10-21: that mode was opened only by the Performance tab’s button, which v14 removed on 2026-09-25 and v17 never drew. A holding is added by a buy on the trade sheet, by Itemize, or by Add an account. To reverse: restore the mode and give it a drawn door.DEC-0647 (v17)
6Every type chip now sets the type. Before, five of them (fixed income, crypto, gold, options, annuities) did nothing when tapped in Edit: they sent you to the bond or alternatives editor, which only the removed add mode could open.A control that does nothing is a defect. Found drawing the sheet, 2026-10-21.—
7Private equity and hedge funds are not offered as types. A holding saved as one before keeps its own chip (state I).His ruling: not offered in this phase; a row saved earlier is kept as it is.DEC-2334
8The Account row shows the account the holding is in, and only that one. Before, every account was offered and choosing another saved nothing, because the holding is not in it.A choice that loses your edits is a defect. Found drawing the sheet, 2026-10-21. A holding moves by a sale in one account and a buy in another.—
9The line warning of a mistyped ticker does not show for a broker’s nine-character security id, the id a bond or a CD carries (states D and E). It still shows for a ticker that cannot be one (state G).The account page names such a holding by its name for the same reason. Found drawing the sheet, 2026-10-22.Build-43 finding #4
10An option asks its shares per contract, 100 unless changed, and counts contracts (state C). A bond held in the account asks its face value, rate, how often it pays and its maturity (state D); a CD is never asked how often it pays (state E).Drawn as built.DEC-4646 · DEC-0281 · DEC-6212 · DEC-3390
11A holding in an account whose total you typed says the holding is tracked for performance and does not change that total (state B).Drawn as built.B-60
122026-10-22 · v02. That line names the tab as the canon names it, Net worth, with a small w. v01 is in archive/, dated 2026-10-22.One term per concept: the canon’s row 9 now retires the capital W wherever it is written.canon row 9
13A holding with no purchase on record shows one blank purchase dated today, and Save waits for a share count (state H).The fresh state of this sheet.DEC-0470
14Held word for word, in order, with every line’s size, weight and colour, by src/screens/__tests__/sheets_drawn_from_code.test.tsx (the holding-editor block). A change to the sheet that is not made here first turns that test red.A drawing nobody checks drifts from the app.—
