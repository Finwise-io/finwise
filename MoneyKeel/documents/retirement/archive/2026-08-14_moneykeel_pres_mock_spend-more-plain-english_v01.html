“4 out of 5” instead of “80%”. Counts are understood by people who bounce off percentages, and the sentence names what happens in the fifth case rather than leaving her to imagine it. No “odds”, no “held at”, no floor to explain.
The sentence changes as she moves the control, because the real number does. Measured on the engine — nothing here is a design flourish:
