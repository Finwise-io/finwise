The rule, in one line. Below the floor, every surface prints the honest sentence instead of the number — and the number is not shown anywhere, in any size.
  1. The floor is 5%. Zero is the obvious trigger, but 1% and 3% are just as useless to read. Say the word if you want it tighter or looser.
  2. All four surfaces — Plan, Home, Net worth, and the sentence a screen reader speaks.
  3. Order: the honest sentence, then the free routes, then the fee-only hourly planner.
Say this out loud, because it is a departure. Everywhere else, this app shows the true number. Here we are replacing a true number with prose. I think it is right — "0%" is accurate and useless, and the sentence is accurate and actionable — but it is the one place we soften something, and it should be a decision made on purpose.
Every figure here was measured, not invented.

New engine work this needs: the earliest age that works, and the spending level that works from it. Neither exists today — the current levers only test retiring two years later and spending $400 less a month, which is why this person got an empty box.

One thing I have not decided for you. The free routes are drawn as categories — a benefits check, Medicare counselling, debt counselling. Naming the actual organisations makes them useful and is safe ground for non-profits and government programmes, unlike naming a firm. But it is your call whether the app names anyone at all. No commercial referrals and no named advice firms in v1 — that is a different regulatory world and would need disclosure.
