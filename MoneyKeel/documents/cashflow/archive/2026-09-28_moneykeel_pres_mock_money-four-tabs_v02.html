MoneyKeel · ONE cash-flow surface, four tabs · v02 · 2026-09-26 · supersedes v01 (archived the same day)
What changed from v01, and why. The founder, 2026-09-26: "fix cashflow screens before moving forward." v01 is the architecture decision of 2026-07-19 — four tabs on one surface, the separate Budget screen retired — and that decision stands and shipped. What had gone stale are the names: v01 was drawn fourteen months of rulings ago. Every frame, figure and card is v01; the words now match the app and the canon.
  • The four tabs are This month · Income · Expenses · Debts. v01 called the first one "Cash flow" (the same words as the screen it sits on) and the third "Spending" (retired, canon row 3). The code has read these four since the surface shipped — src/screens/CashFlowScreen.tsx.
  • The screen is titled Cash flow, not Money. The tab is Cash flow in src/constants/tabs.ts, and a screen carries its tab's name.
  • The bottom bar's third tab is Performance, renamed from Invest on 2026-08-04 (canon row 9). Its route key is still invest, which no person ever sees.
  • The two flow rows name what is inside them (canon row 5): Inflow (income (take-home)) and Outflow (expenses + debt) — the exact strings the screen builds. "In (take-home)" and "Out (bills + debt)" are retired.
  • The category plan is the Budget (canon row 7): the card reads YOUR BUDGET and its link Set your budget › — the words on the budget editor itself.
  • The ledger rows are activity (canon row 12): All activity · search ›, not "All transactions".
  • The income door is the one door: Add your income › from src/constants/doors.ts, not this screen's own "＋ Add or set up income ›".
  • Debt, payments and rates: the card reads YOUR DEBT (canon row 10), payments are $310/month (row 14), and the two rates show two decimals — 22.90% and 6.40% — under the precision ruling Q49.
  • The one month switcher is drawn — ‹ July 2026 › under the title, with the month on the right of the title row. It steers all four tabs and v01 showed it on none of them.
  • The MoneyKeel bar is on every frame — ▦ Menu · the wordmark · the hide-balances eye and the gear. It is the navigator header on every tab screen and v01 drew none of it. Titles are 20pt.
  • "Same functions underneath", not "same engines": "a word invented for something that does not exist in the code" (identifier standard, enforced in copy 2026-09-13).
Nothing else moved. Same four frames, same figures ($1,700 planned surplus, $6,200 in, $4,500 out, $13,000 of debt), same radar, same research note.
Where this sits now. This file is the architecture of the surface — which tabs exist and what belongs on each. The detail of two of those tabs is drawn in moneykeel_pres_mock_cash-flow-this-month-and-expenses_v07.html (This month, Expenses) and of a third in moneykeel_pres_mock_income-end-to-end_v07.html (Income). Where they differ, the detail mock wins — it is newer and was approved on its own.

This month · Income · Expenses · Debts — one screen, no duplication

Your four jobs, one surface. Budget merges into Cash flow (plan-vs-actual IS the cash-flow story — the duplication you caught dies there); Activity becomes Expenses with a big-ticket radar; Income and Debts get their own tabs. The separate Budget screen retires. Same functions underneath — this is consolidation, not new math.
