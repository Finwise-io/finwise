Your base pay — one full screen, one section per person

Redrawn as a full screen. It was a bottom sheet capped at 88% of the display, and three figures plus a fourteen-month table plus a partner’s block was never going to fit in one. Onboarding pushes the same screen, so the questions cannot drift apart in two places.

Each person’s section opens on its own. Collapsed, a section is one line: who, and what lands. Open it and the pay pattern, the three figures and the month table are all there. Two people never have to be on screen at once, and nothing is hidden behind a screen you have to leave to reach.

The rules underneath

1 · One screen, not a sheet. A bottom sheet caps at 88% of the display and scrolls inside itself; three figures, a fourteen-month table and a second person do not belong in one.
2 · One component, two frames. Onboarding pushes the same screen with a progress bar and Continue; Cash flow → Income opens it with a back arrow and Save. Two copies of the same questions is how they come to be asked two different ways.
3 · One section per person, opening on its own. Closed, a section is one line: who, how they are paid, what lands. The screen stays the length of one person’s questions however many people there are.
4 · Each person has their own frequency and their own steady-or-varies answer, and their month table lives inside their own section.
5 · The household total is always visible without opening anything, and says which band it used and why.
