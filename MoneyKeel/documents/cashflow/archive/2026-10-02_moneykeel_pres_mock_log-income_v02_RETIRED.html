WHAT THIS IS

The Log income screen exactly as built on 2026-10-02, after it was audited against the screen standard (moneykeel_pres_spec_screen-design-standard_v01.md, sections 2 and 5) and the gaps fixed. His word of 2026-10-02 — "ok. open them from build but make sure it meets new design standards" — is the approval; the audit is this file. Every string is what src/screens/IncomeScreen.tsx renders, in render order, at the StyleSheet's size, weight and colour; the app bar is TopBar.tsx with ‹ Back.

WHAT CHANGED FROM v01 (2026-10-02, drawn before the audit)

  • The empty state reads "Nothing entered yet." (Q48), then its door.
  • A logged date read a day early west of UTC (new Date on a YYYY-MM-DD string); it reads the local day now.
