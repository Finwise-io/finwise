The rules underneath

1 · Every row shown is a row deducted. Nothing appears on This month without being in the sum — that was the error in the sheet, and it is what the surplus test will pin.
2 · One name, one place. A card payment you enter yourself is a credit card debt payment, entered on the Debts tab, and it reaches This month through the big-ticket radar — the same road the HELOC takes. It is never called “manually entered” on a money row.
3 · A card payment is counted once. Connected card → the imported purchases are the expense, the payment is a radar note only. Not connected → the payment IS the expense.
4 · Recurring debt is never a radar item. Installment debt (mortgage, car) is the same every month; revolving (credit card, HELOC) and a due-in-full lump always are.
5 · The radar never deducts. It is a lens on rows already counted, and each item says whether it is counted.
6 · Nothing is guessed. No plan set → “not set”, never $0 dressed as an answer.
7 · Five, then the rest on a tap. Every list of things that happened shows the five most recent with its full count and total on the closing row, so the number is never hidden behind the fold — only the detail is.
8 · It adds up on the page. 5000−2000−250−2000−200−200 = 350 · 5000−2000−0−500−1200−200−200 = 900 · 200+900+200+700 = 2000 · 500+1200+200+200 = 2100.
