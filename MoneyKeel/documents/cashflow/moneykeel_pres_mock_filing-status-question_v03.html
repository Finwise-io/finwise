How do you file your taxes?

A new onboarding step, straight after “Do you manage money with a spouse?” — because someone who has just answered that is already thinking about the household.

Why it is worth one question. Nothing in the app has ever set a filing status. The code reads one, finds nothing, and falls back to single — for everybody, including the couple who just told us they have a partner. Five surfaces use it: every month’s withholding rate, the effective rate, the Roth conversion, the retirement tax rate, and now the base-pay estimate. On $84,000 a married couple is being shown $328 a month less than lands in their bank.

The rules underneath

1 · Asked in onboarding, straight after the spouse question — not on Income and not on Plan. Four of the five surfaces that need it are live long before anyone opens Plan, and someone who never opens Plan would be taxed as single for ever.
2 · Pre-answered from what they already told us. A spouse means married filing jointly is offered; no spouse means single. Most people confirm rather than choose.
3 · Married filing separately is its own table, not a label on top of single. Every band and the deduction are half the joint figures — the statutory rule, so the two can never drift apart. It happens to match single below $384,350 of taxable income, but filing separately also blocks a Roth contribution above $10,000 and changes how much Social Security is taxed, and the app must know which it is when it models those.
4 · The over-65 question is only asked of a couple. For one person the birth year already answers it. Today the code counts the person alone even on a joint return — a limitation recorded in the 2026-08-20 brief as conservative; this is the moment it can be closed.
5 · No small print on the four options. Anyone who files a return knows what these mean; explaining them reads as talking down. [Founder 2026-08-27]
6 · Say whose income the bracket came from. “Your tax bracket is calculated from your income only. Add your spouse’s income if you want it to be accurate.” Wherever a married-filing-jointly take-home figure appears, not only here. [Founder 2026-08-27]
7 · The over-65 line keeps its explanation. The four filing statuses are things people know; that the deduction rises by $1,650 each at 65 is not.
