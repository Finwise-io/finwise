Cash flow when retired — This month

The tab a retired person opens: what their month brings to spend (‘YOUR PAYCHECK’, canon row 26), how much of it is spent so far, which accounts the draw comes from, the twelve months ahead, and the will-my-money-last strip that lives in the Plan. Phone A is a retired household with savings and Social Security; phone B is a retired person who has entered nothing yet. The figures add up: $2,400 of Social Security + $2,025 drawn from savings = $4,425 this month; $640 + $410 = $1,050 spent, 1,050 ÷ 4,425 = 24% of it, on the 27th of a 31-day month, 87% of the month gone.

WHAT THIS IS

Workplan row 360 (2026-10-26, CFR-5’s tenet-6 checks): ‘The retired This month is on no drawing of record — draw it from the build; no approval of it is on record.’ Every string below is what the build renders on 2026-10-27 for each household, read off the rendered screen; the MoneyKeel bar is TopBar.tsx with Menu; the tab bar is the navigator’s. Phone A’s household: born 1956, retired, Social Security $2,400/month received, $20,000 in checking, a $100,000 brokerage and a $300,000 401(k) set aside for retirement, a $3,000 monthly budget with $1,200 of car insurance in December, and $640 + $410 logged this month.
