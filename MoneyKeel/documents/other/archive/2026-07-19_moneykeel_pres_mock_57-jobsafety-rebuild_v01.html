Job safety — what changes and why

Today this screen has smiley-face answer options, chatty pop-up alerts, an example $2,500/month silently presented as the user's real number, and its answer buried in a same-size table row. The rebuild:

  • 1
    Calm word cards replace the smiley radios. Selection is ✓ + bold — the same language as every toggle in the app now.
  • 2
    The gap is the single hero. "Still needed: $8,400" at 34pt in warning amber, the math spelled out in one readable sentence beneath it, months as finger-sized chips. Was: "Still needed" rendered identically to the input rows; the balances it ran on were 11pt gray.
  • 3
    Honest numbers, real action. The $300/month pace is labeled an example; with no spending logged the screen asks for a number instead of assuming $2,500. It ends with "Save it as a goal ›" instead of a chatty alert.
