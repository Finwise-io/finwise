C · Decisions for the founder

The questionWhat is true todayThe choice
Tips and Rewards are strandedTIPS shows short money tips; REWARDS shows points and streaks. Both were written for a younger audience, both were trimmed from the menu on 2026-08-02, and nothing has linked either since. JOB SAFETY — how long your money holds if the income stops — was collateral: Tips was one of its two doors, so Settings is now the only way in. Retire all three the way /income was (recorded keeps, with the reason), or give them doors. Job safety already has a proposed home in Prepare.
/savings can only be opened during onboardingThe "savings engine" from the onboarding summary — a rate-and-target calculator. Its only link is that summary, so once a person finishes onboarding they can never open it again. It is not in the stranded list because a link does exist; it is simply unreachable in practice. Give it a door, fold what it does into Cash flow, or record it as retired.
Where does monthly income belong?MONTHLY INCOME is where Social Security, a pension and an annuity get entered — the sources behind a retiree’s paycheck. It is linked from four Plan-side screens, but it captures income rather than answering a retirement question. Cash flow, which is where income lives (my lean), or keep it in Plan · Retire.
Does Plan really need three sub-tabs?Retire is the entire redesign — nine screens. Goals is three, Prepare is six, and both are existing screens being regrouped rather than rebuilt. Three sub-tabs, or one Retire surface with Goals and Prepare left in the ☰ menu.
Eight screens have exactly one doorBig costs, will-it-last, credit, itemize, expense, insights, paycheck-months and settings. Each is one deleted link from being unreachable — precisely how big costs broke last week. Accept it, now that the guard catches it the moment it happens, or add a second door to the ones that matter most.
How to trust this page. The top half is generated, so it cannot drift from the code — if a screen loses its last door, this page says so the next time it is run. The middle and bottom halves are written by hand and change only when someone decides something.
