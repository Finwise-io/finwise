# FinWise — User Guide (by who you are)

A plain-English guide organized around **what you're trying to do**, so you can test feature-by-feature.
Bottom tabs are **Home · Budget · Retire · Goals**. Many tools open from cards inside **Goals & Debt**
(the planning hub) or **Net Worth** (reached from the top-bar chip / the grid menu ▦).

**Three global settings (Settings tab):**
- **Simple vs Advisor mode** — Simple hides technical detail (jargon, Monte-Carlo internals, tax math); Advisor shows everything. Start in Simple.
- **Text size** — Default / Large / Larger, applied app-wide.
- **Currency** — pick your currency; every amount reformats instantly.

> Note: most tools below appear **after you finish onboarding**. Run the setup wizard once, then they unlock with your data.

## 🎓 I'm a college student

**Set it up so it's not all about retirement?**
Pick **"I'm a student."** Goals then lead with *Manage money now* (spending, save for a goal, pay down debt). Keep **Simple mode** on.

**My pay is hourly and my hours/months vary — how do I enter that?**
On **Base salary**: choose **Hourly** + your **hours/week**. Then under *"Is your base pay the same every month?"* pick **Set by month** — a 12-row table where you enter each month and set **$0 for months you're not working** (summer off, a gap). ("Fill all" copies one figure to every month to start.)

**Where do tips go?**
On the Base salary screen (Hourly only) there's a **"Tips, on average (per month)"** field.

**How do I enter scholarships and grants?**
Pick **Scholarship / grant** as a source — **add several**, set each monthly or yearly, choose **which months** it lands, and optionally an **exact day & year** (e.g., aid on Sep 20, 2026).

**Planning to take student loans?**
Add **Student loans** (add several) with amount, the month(s) it disburses, and optional day/year. It shows as **cash arriving** in the bill calendar; add a rate + term and we estimate the **monthly payment after graduation** + total interest. Summary on **Goals & Debt → Student loan outlook**.

**Will I have enough each month — and exactly how much to ask my parents, by when?**
Open **Bill calendar** (card on Home). It runs **12 months forward** (money-in vs bills-out, running balance) and flags tight months. The **"Coming up — what to line up"** card answers the real question for a dated bill: *"Tuition $15,000 due Sep 15 — by Sep 13 you'll have ~$1,800. Ask your family for ~$13,200 by Sep 3."*

**How do I budget tuition, meal plans, books?**
In the spending plan they're grouped by priority (**Critical / Important / Nice-to-have**); for one-off costs like tuition you pick **which month(s)** + an optional **due day**.

**How do I build credit?**
**Goals & Debt → Build credit** — enter card balance + limit for your **utilization** (keep under 30%), optionally a score band, and the five habits that move it.

**Save for a trip or big purchase?**
**Goals tab → Add a goal** — set the amount + a **target date (MM/YYYY)** and we back-calc the monthly amount.

## 🍽️ My income changes week to week (server, gig, hourly, freelance)

**My income isn't the same each month — how do I capture that?**
On **Base salary**, pick **Set by month** and enter each month's pay (uneven is fine; $0 for a gap). This drives every projection — no averaging.

**Where do tips go?**
The **"Tips (avg per month)"** field on the Base salary screen (Hourly). Tips count in your totals and cash flow only in months you're working.

**1099 / side work?**
Add **Self-employment / consulting** as a source (monthly or yearly).

**Will I make it through a lean stretch?**
The **Bill calendar** uses your actual month-by-month income, so lean months show as real dips in the running balance and get flagged as tight.

**How big should my emergency fund be — and could I absorb a surprise?**
**Net Worth tab → runway** ("your cash covers ~N months"). Tap **"Stress-test an emergency"** → the **What-if** screen: pick a shock (medical/car/job-loss or custom) and see if your cash covers it, your **job-loss runway**, and a 3-month fund target.

**Paycheck to paycheck — which bills first?**
Spending is tiered **Critical → Important → Nice-to-have**; the bill calendar's **"pay these first"** protects essentials when short.

**Build credit / chip away at a card?**
**Goals & Debt → Build credit** + the **debt payoff plan** (avalanche/snowball).

## 💻 I'm a working professional (salary, bonus, equity)

**Capture salary + bonus + RSUs?**
Add **Employment**: base salary (with the same-every-month or **set-by-month** table), **bonus** (+ which month it lands), **signing bonus**, and **equity/RSUs** with a vesting schedule.

**How much 401(k) room is left?**
**Insights** (from Home) surfaces remaining **401(k) headroom** ("you can still contribute $X — tax-advantaged").

**How's my portfolio doing vs the market?**
**Net Worth → Portfolio performance vs benchmark.** Pick a period (1M–3Y); compares **your return vs the matching index over the same period**, with per-holding breakdowns, allocation, and a trend line. Adding a holding? The **ticker field auto-suggests** common symbols and sets the right benchmark.

**Tax if I sold stock?**
On **Performance**, the **"If you sold everything now"** card estimates **capital-gains tax**, split **long-term (>1yr, ~15%)** vs **short-term**.

**Should I convert pre-tax savings to Roth?**
**Goals & Debt → Roth conversion** — in a low-income year, see how much you can convert to "fill" a tax bracket, the tax cost, and the rate.

**Am I on track to retire? What if I get raises / retire earlier?**
**Retire tab** shows your odds ("lasts to 90 in X% of scenarios"). The **scenario sandbox** has sliders for retirement age, monthly saving, return, inflation, and **pay raises/year** → **"Use as my plan."**

**Pay off debt or invest?**
**Goals & Debt** — your **debt-to-income ratio** + an avalanche/snowball **payoff plan** (and what extra payments save); Home shows what's free to save.

**Net worth + where my money sits?**
**Net Worth tab** (donut: cash / investments / retirement / property − debts). A **net-worth-over-time** chart appears on Home as months accrue.

**Planning for kids' college or protecting my family?**
**Goals & Debt → College planner** (529 cost + monthly target) and **→ Life insurance check** (coverage gap, prefilled from your income + debts).

## 👴 I'm retired (or nearly)

**Enter Social Security, pension, withdrawals?**
Pick **"I'm retired"**; the **Retirement income** screen captures **SS, pension, 401(k)/IRA withdrawals, RMDs, annuities** — counted in your income recap, cash flow, and projection.

**Will my money last?**
**Retire tab → "Will it last?"** — a deterministic estimate plus ~400 market simulations through your plan-to age.

**Safe withdrawal rate? Which accounts first? RMDs?**
The Retire tab shows your **withdrawal rate** vs ~4%, the **age your money could run out**, a **suggested withdrawal order** (cash → taxable → pre-tax), and **RMDs**.

**Big medical / care cost in retirement?**
On the retirement setup, your **medical budget** and **"will spending change later?"** now feed the projection's spending.

**Leave money to family / get my affairs in order?**
At setup, retirees lead with **Plan ahead** goals. **Goals & Debt → Estate & legacy** is a saved checklist (will, beneficiaries, power of attorney, healthcare directive, guardian, documents).

**Make everything easier to read?**
**Settings → Text size → Large / Larger.**

## 🧰 Planning & tax tools (all under Goals & Debt)

Each is a card on the **Goals & Debt** screen:
- **Build credit** — utilization calculator (balance ÷ limit, 30% marker), score band, habits.
- **College planner** — 529: projected cost (inflated), on-track %, monthly to close the gap, "save as a goal."
- **Life insurance check** — DIME coverage gap, prefilled from your income + debts, with a need→covered→gap breakdown.
- **Estate & legacy** — a saved checklist with a progress %.
- **Roth conversion** — fill-a-bracket: amount to convert, tax cost, effective rate.
- **Tax organizer** — a year-end summary for your accountant: income by source (taxable vs tax-free), retirement contributions, year-end accounts, and a **document checklist** tailored to your situation (W-2, 1099s, 1098s…). **"Share as PDF"** (works in the installed app build).

Also on Goals & Debt: **debt-to-income ratio**, **student-loan outlook**, **avalanche/snowball payoff plan**, **goals** (with target dates), and a **sinking-fund** suggestion for non-monthly costs.

## 🧭 Everyone — setup, money basics & settings

**Add accounts and debts?**
**Net Worth tab** — cash / investment / retirement / property accounts, plus debts (rate, minimum, due day). Net worth updates live. *(The Budget "Debts" tab and Net Worth now share one debt list.)*

**Log income, spending, a debt payment?**
**Budget tab** — Transactions / Budget / Debts / Import. Or **"+ Add expense"** on Home.

**Add a recurring annual bill (e.g., life-insurance premium)?**
In the spending plan, add a **custom category** and toggle it **Yearly** (vs Monthly) — then pick the month(s) it's due and an optional due day. The budget spreads it correctly and the bill calendar lands it in its month.

**See/edit everything I entered for income?**
**Income manager** lists every source with totals; **Income detail** shows gross/net, effective tax rate, and a month-by-month cash-flow chart. Both now show **actual dividends/interest** from your holdings when you have them (instead of the estimate).

**What do Insights & "Sharpen your plan" do?**
**Insights** — a ranked to-do (emergency fund, 401(k) room, over-concentration, savings rate). **Sharpen your plan** — a checklist of skipped setup steps; finishing them sharpens every number.

**Rewards? Job-loss plan?**
**Rewards tab** (XP + badges). **Tips → Job safety check** builds a safety plan.

**Currency / text size / detail level?**
**Settings tab** — Currency picker, Text size, Simple/Advisor mode, budget/pay frequency.

**Is my data safe?**
Stored encrypted on-device and synced to your account for recovery; verify your email under Settings.

### Notes
- Prices are **end-of-day** from a free source during development; past performance isn't a guarantee.
- Tax and projection numbers are **estimates** to plan around — not advice or a filed return.
- **PDF export** (Tax organizer) and **receipt scanning** run in the installed app build, not the Expo Go preview.
- The income insight reads like *"About $19k most months — extra in Dec; lower in May–Jul"* once your pay varies by month.
