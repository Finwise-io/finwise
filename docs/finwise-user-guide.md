# FinWise — User Guide (by who you are)

A plain-English guide organized around **what you're trying to do**. Find the section that sounds most
like you, then scan the questions. Most answers point you to a specific screen — the bottom tabs are
**Home · Budget · Retire · Goals**, and other screens open from cards inside those.

Two settings change how the whole app feels (Settings tab):
- **Simple vs Advisor mode** — Simple hides the technical detail (jargon, Monte Carlo, tax internals); Advisor shows everything. Start in Simple.
- **Text size** — Default / Large / Larger, applied everywhere.
- **Currency** — pick your currency and every amount reformats.

## 🎓 I'm a college student

**How do I set this up without it feeling like it's built for rich older people?**
On the first screen pick **"I'm a student."** Your goals then lead with *Manage money now* (track spending, save for a goal, pay down debt) — not retirement. Leave it in **Simple mode** and the investing/retirement detail stays out of your way.

**My pay is hourly and I don't work 40 hours — how do I enter that?**
On the income setup, choose **hourly** and enter your **hours per week** (we don't assume full-time). If the job ends (summer/semester job), mark it **Temporary** and set the **start and end dates** — we only count the months you actually work.

**How do I enter my scholarships and grants?**
Pick **Scholarship / grant** as an income source. You can **add several** (most students have more than one), set each as monthly or yearly, and choose **which months it lands** — so your bill calendar knows aid arrives in, say, August and January.

**I'm planning to take student loans — can I plan around them?**
Yes. Add **Student loans** as a source and enter each loan (you can add several). It shows as **cash arriving** in your bill calendar in the months you pick, and if you add an interest rate + term we estimate your **monthly payment after graduation** and total interest. The full picture is on **Goals & Debt → Student loan outlook**.

**Will I have enough money each month, and when will it be tight?**
Open **Bill calendar** (card on Home). It lays out money-in vs bills-out month by month with a running balance, and flags the months you'd come up **short** (e.g., before aid lands). If you can't cover everything, it shows **which bills to pay first**.

**How do I budget tuition, meal plans, and books?**
In your spending plan those are first-class categories, grouped by priority (**Critical / Important / Nice-to-have**), and for big one-off costs like tuition you pick **which month(s)** they're due.

**How do I build credit?**
**Goals & Debt → Build credit.** Enter your card balance and limit to see your **utilization** (keep it under 30%), optionally check your **score band**, and read the five habits that actually move your score.

**How do I save up for a trip or a big purchase?**
**Goals tab → Add a goal.** Set the amount and a **target date (month/year)** and we back-calculate how much to set aside each month.

## 🍽️ My income changes week to week (server, gig, hourly, freelance)

**My income isn't the same each month — how do I tell the app that?**
On the income/salary screen, set **"Is your income steady or does it vary?"** to **It varies**, then enter roughly what you bring in **in a slow month**. We plan around the lean stretches instead of assuming a flat paycheck.

**Where do my tips go?**
There's a **"Tips, on average (per month)"** field on the salary screen. Tips are counted as (taxable) income across all your totals and your cash flow.

**How do I know if I'll survive a slow month?**
Open **Bill calendar** and use the **Typical month / Slow month** toggle. Slow month re-runs your whole year on your lean-month income so you can see exactly when you'd run short — and how big a cushion you need.

**I live paycheck to paycheck — which bills matter most?**
Your spending is grouped **Critical (rent, food, utilities) → Important → Nice-to-have**, and the bill calendar's **"pay these first"** list protects the essentials when money's tight.

**How big should my emergency fund be?**
**Net Worth tab** shows a **runway** line — "your cash covers ~N months of spending." Aim for 3–6 months; it's color-coded so you can see at a glance.

**I do some 1099 / side work — where does that go?**
Add **Self-employment / consulting** as an income source (enter it monthly or yearly).

**How do I build credit or chip away at a card?**
**Goals & Debt → Build credit** for utilization + habits, and the **debt payoff plan** on the same screen shows the fastest/cheapest way to clear balances.

## 💻 I'm a working professional (salary, bonus, equity)

**How do I capture salary + bonus + RSUs?**
Add **Employment** and you'll get screens for base salary, **bonus** (and which month it lands — not everyone gets it in December), **signing bonus**, and **equity/RSUs** with a vesting schedule. Each flows into your income and cash flow correctly.

**How much can I still put in my 401(k) this year?**
Check **Insights** (from Home) — it surfaces remaining **401(k) headroom** ("you can still contribute $X this year — tax-advantaged").

**How is my portfolio doing versus the market?**
**Net Worth → Portfolio performance vs benchmark.** Pick a period (1M–3Y); it compares **your return vs the matching index over the same period** (apples-to-apples), with per-holding breakdowns, allocation, and a trend line.

**If I sold some stock, what would I owe in tax?**
On the **Performance** screen, the **"If you sold everything now"** card estimates your **capital-gains tax**, split into **long-term (held >1 year, ~15%)** vs **short-term (taxed as income)** — a reminder that holding past a year is taxed less.

**Am I on track to retire?**
**Retire tab.** It shows your odds in plain terms ("your money lasts to age 90 in X% of scenarios") from a Monte-Carlo simulation, plus your projected balance.

**What if I get raises, save more, or retire earlier?**
In the Retire tab's **scenario sandbox**, drag sliders for retirement age, monthly saving, expected return, inflation, and **pay raises per year** — then **"Use as my plan"** to keep it.

**Should I pay off debt or invest?**
**Goals & Debt** shows your **debt-to-income ratio**, an avalanche/snowball **payoff plan** (and what extra payments save), while Home shows how much you have free to save — so you can weigh both.

**What's my net worth and where's my money sitting?**
**Net Worth tab** — a donut of cash / investments / retirement / property minus debts, with every account listed and a **net-worth-over-time** chart on Home as the months accrue.

## 👴 I'm retired (or nearly)

**How do I enter Social Security, a pension, and my withdrawals?**
Pick **"I'm retired"** at setup; your **Retirement income** screen captures **Social Security, pension, 401(k)/IRA withdrawals, RMDs, and annuities**. These now count in your income recap **and** your bill calendar — not just the projection.

**Will my money last?**
**Retire tab → "Will it last?"** A deterministic estimate plus ~400 market simulations tell you whether your savings last through your plan-to age, and in what share of scenarios.

**Am I withdrawing at a safe rate?**
The Retire tab shows your **withdrawal rate** against the ~4% guideline, and the **age your money could run out** at your current spend.

**Which accounts should I spend down first, and what about RMDs?**
There's a **suggested withdrawal order** (e.g., cash → taxable → pre-tax), and **RMDs** are part of the retirement-income picture.

**How do I plan to leave money to family or a cause?**
At setup, retirees lead with **Plan ahead** goals including **"Leave money to family or a cause."**

**Can I make everything easier to read?**
**Settings → Text size → Large or Larger** enlarges text across the whole app.

**Is my net worth holding up over time?**
**Net Worth tab** for the full picture; the **net-worth-over-time** card on Home tracks the trend month to month.

## 🧭 Everyone — setup, money basics & settings

**How do I add my accounts and debts?**
**Net Worth tab.** Add cash, investment, retirement, and property accounts, plus debts (with rate, minimum, and due day). Your net worth updates live.

**How do I log income, spending, or a debt payment?**
**Budget tab** — add transactions, and switch between Transactions / Budget / Debts / Import. You can also tap **"+ Add expense"** on Home.

**How do I see and edit everything I entered for income?**
**Income manager** (from Budget/Home) lists every source you entered with totals; **Income detail** shows your gross/net, effective tax rate, and a month-by-month cash-flow chart.

**What do "Insights" and "Sharpen your plan" do?**
**Insights** is a ranked list of the most useful things to act on right now (emergency fund, 401(k) room, over-concentration, savings rate). **Sharpen your plan** is a checklist of setup steps you skipped — finishing them makes every number more accurate.

**Do I earn anything for using it?**
**Rewards tab** — XP and badges for logging income/spending, setting goals, and keeping a streak.

**Worried about losing your job?**
**Job safety check** (from Tips) asks a couple of questions and builds a safety plan.

**How do I change currency, text size, or detail level?**
**Settings tab** — Currency picker, Text size, and **Simple/Advisor** display mode, plus budget/pay frequency.

**Is my data safe?**
Your data is stored encrypted on your device and synced to your account so you can recover it; verify your email under Settings to enable recovery.

### Notes
- Market prices are **end-of-day** from a free source during development; past performance isn't a guarantee.
- Tax and projection figures are **estimates** to plan around, not advice or exact filings.
- Some screens (bill calendar, retirement detail, credit) appear once you've **completed onboarding** — finish the setup wizard to unlock them with your data.
