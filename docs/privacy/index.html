FinWise ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FinWise mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your name and email address.

Financial data you enter: Income entries, expenses, savings, investments, debts, goals, and budget settings you create within the app. This data is stored on your device and optionally synced to your private Firestore account.

Receipt images: If you use the receipt scanning feature, images are sent to Google Cloud Vision API for text extraction. Images are not stored by FinWise after processing.

Feedback: If you submit in-app feedback, we collect the content of your message along with your name and email.

Usage data: We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking beyond what Firebase Authentication records for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your financial data across your devices
  • To generate personalized AI-powered financial insights (via Anthropic's Claude API)
  • To display live economic data (inflation rate, Treasury yield) from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and U.S. Treasury APIs
  • To respond to feedback and support requests
  • To improve the app based on aggregated, anonymized feedback

3. Third-Party Services

FinWise uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and cloud data sync. Your financial data is stored in your private Firestore document, accessible only to you. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud Vision API: Receipt OCR. Receipt images are transmitted for text extraction only and are not retained. Google Privacy Notice
  • Anthropic Claude API: AI-generated expense tips and financial insights. Spending summaries (no personally identifiable information) are sent to generate suggestions. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics API: Live inflation data. No personal data is transmitted.
  • U.S. Treasury API: Live Treasury yield data. No personal data is transmitted.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device using encrypted AsyncStorage and optionally synced to Google Firestore. Firestore security rules ensure that only you — authenticated as yourself — can read or write your data. We do not have access to your individual financial records.

We use industry-standard security practices, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password for your account.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Reset all data within the app. To permanently delete your account and all associated data from our servers, email us at support@finwise.app and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

FinWise is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@finwise.app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
