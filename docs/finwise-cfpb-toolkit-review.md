# CFPB "Your Money, Your Goals" toolkit — review & how it shapes FinWise

Source: 16 CFPB financial-empowerment tools the user shared (public-domain, US Bureau of Consumer
Financial Protection). Audience: people with **limited financial knowledge / limited literacy / ELL** —
i.e. our **Simple mode** and **student / low-income** users. Use this to shape the simple + student
experience and the income redesign.

## What the toolkit covers (and our status)

| CFPB tool | Core idea | FinWise status |
|---|---|---|
| Income & benefits tracker | Income incl. **benefits** (SNAP, TANF, disability, child support), weekly; mark regular vs irregular/seasonal/one-time | ⚠️ income yes, **benefits NO** |
| Cash flow budget | **Weekly** beginning balance → +income −expenses → ending balance, carried week to week | ❌ we're monthly only |
| Bill calendar | Calendar of **due dates** vs income dates; avoid late fees | ❌ no due-date tracking |
| Prioritizing bills | When you can't pay all: rank by consequence (job → insurance → housing → obligations) | ❌ |
| Spending tracker | Month of receipts by category; circle recurring = needs | ✅ budget by category |
| Cutting expenses | Per-category checklist of ways to spend less | ➕ could feed insights |
| Debt log | List every debt incl. **past-due bills, medical, family, court-ordered** (a bill isn't debt unless past-due) | ⚠️ debts yes, narrower |
| Debt-to-income calculator | Monthly debt ÷ gross income; guideline renters 15–20%, homeowners ≤36% | ❌ |
| Debt action plan | Snowball vs avalanche | ✅ payoffPlan |
| Planning for life events & large purchases | Brainstorm future purchases (car, college, baby, wedding) → timeframe, cost, save vs borrow | ✅ sinking-fund + goals (partial) |
| Savings plan | Goal ÷ **weeks** = weekly target + find-the-money strategies | ⚠️ we do monthly-to-target |
| Saving & asset limits | Public-benefit asset limits (SSI/SNAP/TANF/Medicaid) — saving too much can cut benefits | ❌ (niche but important) |
| My money picture | 10 yes/no intake → routes to the right starting topic | ➕ could enhance onboarding/Sharpen |
| Financial empowerment self-assessment | Knowledge quiz + feelings + experiences | ➕ "Learn" content |
| Finding financial products & services | Goal → product glossary (checking, CD, credit-builder, secured card…) | ➕ "Learn" content |
| How to handle identity theft | Handout: freeze, fraud alert, FTC | ➕ "Learn"/Protect content |

## What this tells us about Simple mode (design principles)
- **Plain language, no jargon.** Dollars over percentages. Short sentences. One question per screen (we do).
- **Read-aloud friendly** (ELL / low literacy): big text (we have large-text), simple words, clear icons.
- **Weekly thinking**, not annual: "will I get through this week/month?" beats 30-year projections for this user.
- **Benefits are income.** For students/low-income, SNAP/TANF/disability/housing-subsidy/child-support are core — and some are **restricted-use** with **asset limits**.
- **Timing matters**: due dates vs paydays; lumpy/irregular income.
- **Triage first**: "where do you need help most" → start there (My money picture).

## Recommended adoption — prioritized

**Tier 1 — fold into work already in flight / high value for simple+student**
1. **Benefits as an income source** (SNAP, TANF, unemployment, disability, housing subsidy, child support, scholarships/grants). Folds directly into the income-sources picker. Flag restricted-use; note asset-limit caveat as an insight.
2. **Weekly cash-flow "Get through the month" view** — beginning balance → income − expenses → ending, week by week. The toolkit's centerpiece; a strong Simple-mode home.
3. **Bill calendar + due dates** (avoid late fees) and **Prioritize-bills-when-short** triage.

**Tier 2 — small, high-leverage**
4. **Debt-to-income ratio** (with renter/homeowner guideline) + extend the debt log to past-due/medical/family/court-ordered.
5. **Weekly savings target** option on goals (goal ÷ weeks).
6. **Cut-expenses suggestions** in the insight engine.

**Tier 3 — "Learn" / Protect content (lower priority)**
7. Product glossary, identity-theft steps, self-assessment quiz, benefit asset-limit reference, "My money picture" triage.
